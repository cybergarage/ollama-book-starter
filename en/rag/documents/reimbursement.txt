Expense Reimbursement Policy (Sample)

Article 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how employees are reimbursed for business expenses. The General Affairs Department decides matters not covered by this policy as they arise.

Article 2. Submission deadline
Submit an expense claim by the 10th day of the month after the month in which the expense was incurred. Late claims are generally not accepted. If unavoidable circumstances prevent timely submission, attach a written explanation, submit it to the General Affairs Department, and obtain the department manager's approval. Expenses incurred in March, the end of the fiscal year, are due by March 31 rather than in the following month.

Article 3. Submission and retention
Submit claim forms and receipts to the General Affairs Department. If you submit a digitized receipt, keep the original for three months. If a receipt is lost, submit a payment statement stating the date and time, amount, payee, and purpose instead. No more than three claims per month may use a payment statement.

Article 4. Transportation
Public transit fares are reimbursed in full. Limited express train surcharges are allowed only for one-way trips over 100 kilometers. Green Car travel is not allowed. Taxis are allowed only for travel after 10 p.m., transport of heavy items, or travel to an area without public transit. Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at 25 yen per kilometer. Actual parking charges are reimbursed with advance approval from a supervisor.

Article 5. Lodging
For business travel requiring an overnight stay, actual lodging costs are reimbursed up to 10,000 yen per night. The limit is 13,000 yen in Tokyo's 23 wards, Osaka City, and Nagoya City. Lodging costs for overseas travel are governed separately. If breakfast is included in the lodging price, it need not be claimed separately.

Article 6. Meeting expenses
Food and drinks for a meeting with external participants are reimbursed up to 3,000 yen per person. Advance approval from the department manager is required to exceed this limit. Food and drinks for employees only are not reimbursable. List each participant's name and affiliation on the claim form.

Article 7. Supplies
Purchases of stationery, books, and software needed for work are reimbursable. Purchases over 10,000 yen per item require advance approval from a supervisor. Move books to the company bookshelf six months after purchase.

Article 8. Payment
Approved expenses are paid with salary on the 25th day of the following month. If the 25th is a bank holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day.

Article 9. Fraud
If a false claim is discovered, the employee must return the reimbursed amount and may face disciplinary action under the employment rules.
