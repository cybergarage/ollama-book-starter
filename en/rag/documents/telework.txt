Remote Work Guidelines (Sample)

1. Purpose
These guidelines set out the conditions and procedures for remote work. They aim to support employees' flexibility while maintaining the quality of work.

2. Eligibility
Employees become eligible after six months of employment. Employees in their probationary period are not eligible. However, employees with childcare or caregiving needs may consult Human Resources regardless of their start date.

3. Permitted days
Remote work is allowed for up to half of the scheduled workdays in a month. The limit may change according to departmental circumstances. Working remotely beyond the limit requires approval from the department manager and Human Resources.

4. Applying
Register remote work in the attendance system by 5 p.m. on the preceding business day. For a same-day change due to sudden illness or a family matter, contact your direct supervisor before the workday begins. Failure to report an absence from the office will be treated as an absence from work.

5. Working hours
Standard hours are 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Employees must work from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. and may adjust their hours around that period. Record time away during the workday in the attendance system. If total hours for the day fall below the required hours, the difference is treated as paid leave or an absence.

6. Communication
Remain available on chat during working hours. If you will be away for 30 minutes or more, post a message in the team chat. As a rule, join meetings with the camera on.

7. Internet connection
Provide an internet connection sufficient for work. A 200 yen communications allowance is paid for each remote workday. This is added to the following month's salary rather than reimbursed as an expense.

8. Information handling
Do not print confidential materials at home or work where family members can see your screen. Working in public places, such as cafes or coworking spaces, is not allowed. The same rule applies while riding in a vehicle.

9. Equipment
The company lends the equipment used at home. Do not use personal equipment for work. Employees pay for their own desks and chairs. Report any damage to loaned equipment immediately to the Information Systems Department.

10. Review
These guidelines are reviewed annually based on how remote work is being used.
