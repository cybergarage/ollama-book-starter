New Employee Onboarding Procedure (Sample)

Preparation before the first day
By three business days before the employee's start date, the receiving department must secure a desk, arrange loaned equipment, request a building access pass, prepare a first-day schedule, and assign a mentor. Request equipment from the Information Systems Department and the access pass from the General Affairs Department. Arrangements take five business days, so make requests early.

First day's schedule
A staff member greets the employee at the first-floor reception desk at 9:30 a.m. During the morning, the employee receives an access pass and loaned equipment and sets up accounts. The employee has lunch with the mentor. In the afternoon, the employee attends information security training and takes a follow-up test. A meeting with the supervisor at 5 p.m. ends the first day.

First week
The employee learns about the assigned department's work, receives accounts for the systems used, and meets other departments. At the end of the week, the employee meets with the direct supervisor for 30 minutes to discuss questions.

First month
The employee takes responsibility for one task. The mentor holds a 30-minute review each week. The discussion is recorded in the designated form so the supervisor and Human Resources can read it. During this period, the employee must ask the mentor whenever a decision is unclear.

Third month
At the end of the probationary period, the employee meets with the supervisor and Human Resources. They discuss proficiency in assigned tasks, teamwork, and future goals. The result is given to the employee in writing.

Sixth month
The employee becomes eligible for remote work. Those who want to work remotely should read the Remote Work Guidelines and ask their supervisor.

Mentor's role
The mentor teaches work procedures and builds an approachable relationship. In addition to the weekly review, the mentor checks in regularly. The mentor's own workload should be reduced by about 20 percent during the onboarding period.

Contacts
Contact Human Resources about procedures, the Information Systems Department about equipment and accounts, and the General Affairs Department about access passes and desks.
